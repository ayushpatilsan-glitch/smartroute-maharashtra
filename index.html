Why SmartRoute?

A graph-based multimodal route optimization system for Pune & Maharashtra public transport.

🗺️ Multimodal Routes

Bus, Metro, Auto, Train, Taxi & Walking combined. See every viable path, not just one “best” answer.

⚡ Dijkstra’s Algorithm

Shortest distance, fastest time, cheapest fare and minimum transfers — all computed with priority queues.

📊 Compare Everything

Distance, time, fare, transfers and modes side-by-side so you can choose what matters most.

🎓 Built for Learning

Graph, Adjacency List, Hash Table, Min-Heap and path reconstruction clearly implemented and visualized.

Focus Area: Pune Metropolitan Region

Primary demo covers Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad and surrounding hubs. Architecture supports expanding to Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik and more.
